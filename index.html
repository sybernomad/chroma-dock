How to play

  • Swipe a block to slide it. It keeps going until something stops it.
  • Slide a block into the gate of its colour to clear it. Other gates act as walls.
  • A block that lands on an elevator stops there. After every move, elevators step along their track and carry their block.
  • Big blocks (bars, crates, L-blocks) slide as one piece. They need a gate at least as wide as they are, and they can't ride elevators.
  • Locked gates show a padlock. They stay shut until every block of the padlock's colour has left the board.
  • Generators drop a new block whenever their outlet is clear. Every block has to escape.
  • The clock starts on your first move, so take your time to plan. Beat it for bonus gems. Running out of time never ends the level.
  • You can also tap a block, then tap a spot in the same row or column. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys, Z (undo) and R (restart).
